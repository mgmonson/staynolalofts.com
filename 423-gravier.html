The space

Set on the third floor of a boutique warehouse conversion, this fully renovated two-bedroom condo pairs old New Orleans brick and bones with new plumbing, wiring, and finishes. It's built for a traveling professional who wants a real kitchen, a quiet place to work, and a short walk to everything else.

The primary bedroom holds a king bed behind its own door. The second bedroom is an open loft with a queen bed, overlooking the living area below — ideal for a partner, colleague, or older child who still wants some separation. A queen sleeper sofa in the living room adds a third sleeping option when needed.

Neighborhood

Central Business District

Gravier Street sits at the quiet edge of the CBD, close enough to walk to dinner in the French Quarter but far enough to sleep through the night. The St. Charles streetcar line is nearby for anyone commuting toward Uptown, and the building is a short drive from the city's main medical and legal corridors — one reason it's found by traveling nurses, physicians, and attorneys on assignment.
